Privacy Policy

Tofly Dev Test App · Effective 29 October 2026

This app is a personal development and testing app built for a university software engineering course project. It is not an official product or service of Tofly. Only invited sandbox test users can connect to it.

1. Data we read

When you connect your TikTok account, and only with your permission, the app reads:

  • Basic profile information: your TikTok user ID, username and display name (scopes user.info.basic and user.info.profile).
  • The list of videos posted by your own account and their metrics: views, likes, comments and shares (scope video.list).

The app does not read your messages, followers, contacts or any other account's data, and it does not post anything on your behalf.

2. Why we use it

Only to test the connection flow and to check that video performance metrics can be read correctly for a creator performance feature in the course project.

3. How it is stored

  • Data is stored in the developer's development database.
  • Access tokens from TikTok are encrypted before they are saved and are never shown in the browser or written to logs.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

4. How long we keep it

Data is kept only during testing. Tokens are deleted when you disconnect. All test data is deleted when the course project ends, no later than 31 December 2026.

5. How to remove access

  • Disconnect your TikTok account from inside the app. This deletes the stored tokens immediately.
  • Or remove the app's access from the connected apps section in your TikTok account settings.
  • To ask for all of your test data to be deleted, contact us at the address below.
