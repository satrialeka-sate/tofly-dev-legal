1. Who can use the app

Only test accounts that the developer has invited as sandbox target users may use this app. Access is for testing purposes only.

2. What the app does

The app tests connecting a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit and reading the performance metrics (views, likes, comments and shares) of videos posted by that same account. The results are used only to develop and test a creator performance feature for the course project.

3. Your responsibilities
